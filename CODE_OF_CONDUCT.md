# Code of conduct

Epixo is for makers of every level, from a first project to a professional product. Everyone should feel welcome here.

## Expected

- Be kind and patient, especially with beginners.
- Give feedback on the work, not the person.
- Credit other people's work when you build on it.
- Respect different experience, languages and backgrounds.

## Not accepted

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory jokes and language
- Sexual content or unwanted attention
- Publishing someone's private information without permission
- Spam, or repeatedly derailing discussions

## Enforcement

Report a problem by opening an issue, or privately through the **Report content** option on GitHub. The maintainers may remove content, or warn, suspend or ban anyone who breaks these rules, in this repository and in the Epixo community.

Adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org), version 2.1.
